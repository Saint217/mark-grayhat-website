FAQ

Common Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before starting an engagement.

What types of organizations do you typically work with?

I work with businesses of all sizes — from Series A startups to Fortune 500 enterprises — as well as legal teams requiring forensic expertise, healthcare organizations, financial institutions, and government contractors. If you operate in a regulated industry or handle sensitive data, I can help.

How do you handle confidentiality and NDAs?

Every engagement begins with a mutual NDA before any information is exchanged. Client identities and engagement details are never disclosed without explicit permission. All findings are communicated exclusively through secure, encrypted channels.

How long does a typical penetration test take?

Scope determines duration. A focused web application test typically takes 3–5 days. A full enterprise network penetration test may span 2–4 weeks. A scoping call is always the first step to accurately estimate timeline and resource requirements.

Can you assist during an active security breach?

Yes. Incident response is available on an emergency basis, 24/7. If you're facing an active breach, ransomware attack, or data leak, contact me immediately. Response retainers are also available for organizations that want guaranteed SLA-backed response times.

What certifications and qualifications do you hold?

I hold OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional), CEH, CISSP, CISM, and ISO 27001 Lead Auditor certifications, with over 8 years of hands-on experience across financial services, healthcare, legal, and enterprise environments.

Do you provide reports suitable for regulatory auditors or courts?

Yes. Reports are structured to meet regulatory requirements (PCI-DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2) and are written to be accessible to both technical teams and executive stakeholders. Forensic reports follow strict chain-of-custody standards and are admissible in legal proceedings.

What does a typical engagement cost?

Pricing is scope-dependent. A focused vulnerability assessment starts at $3,500. Full-scope penetration tests begin at $8,000. Incident response is billed on a time-and-materials basis. Retainer programs are available for ongoing advisory and priority access. Contact me for a detailed quote.
